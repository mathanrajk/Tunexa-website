Tunexa is available for Android today, and an iOS version is on the way. Update this once it’s live.

What audio formats can I upload?

Common formats like MP3 and AAC work great. Add your exact limits (file size, formats) here.

Can I make my playlists private?

Yes. Each playlist can be public or private, and you can change it any time.

How do I delete my listening history?

Long press tracks in History to remove several, or use “Clear All History” in Settings.
